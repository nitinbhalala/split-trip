01 Information We Collect

Split Trip collects minimal data required to provide seamless expense splitting, multi-account database isolation, and Google Drive cloud backup functionality.

A. Account & Profile Data:

  • Google / Apple Account Info: Email address, full name, and profile picture URL obtained during Google or Apple authentication.
  • User Profile Info: Custom profile name, email, and profile avatar stored locally.

B. App Usage & Expense Data:

  • Trip & Group Data: Group names, member lists, trip descriptions, icon preferences, and selected currency.
  • Expense Data: Payment records, split ratios, amounts, payer details, and transaction dates.
  • Data Storage: All data remains locally on the user's device; the app does not read, write, or store any data on external servers.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following functional purposes:

  • To calculate exact group balances, individual shares, and simplified debt settlements.
  • To maintain multi-account user isolation so switching Google/Apple accounts keeps local data completely segregated.
  • To compress and store custom group images locally on your device without unnecessary cloud bandwidth.
  • To enable user-controlled backup and restore of local SQLite databases via Google Drive.

03 Google Drive API & Data Disclosure

Split Trip integrates with Google Drive API to offer optional automated database backup and restore functionality.

  • Scope Used: Split Trip uses restricted app-data scope (drive.appdata or drive.file).
  • Limited Access: Split Trip ONLY accesses backup files created by the app itself within your personal Google Drive storage space.
  • No Scanning: The app does NOT read, view, scan, modify, or delete any other personal documents, photos, or files in your Google Drive.
  • Google User Data Policy: Split Trip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

04 Storage & Data Security

We prioritize your privacy by storing your sensitive financial and trip split records directly on your local device storage using isolated SQLite database engines.

  • Local Database Isolation: Each logged-in account operates in its own sanitized, separate database file (e.g., split_trip_user.db).
  • Image Storage: Picked group images are automatically compressed (max 512x512) and saved permanently in your app’s private documents folder.
  • Encryption in Transit: Any cloud backup transfer to Google Drive occurs over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.

All data is read and written only on the user's device; no data is transmitted to external servers.

05 Third-Party Services & Libraries

Split Trip integrates trusted third-party SDKs for authentication and platform services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Used to securely sign in users via Google or Apple accounts. Privacy policy: Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services & Drive API: Used for account validation and database backup.
  • Apple Sign-In (Apple Inc.) via Firebase: Used for authentication on iOS devices through Firebase Authentication. Privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy.

06 Data Retention & Your Rights (Data Deletion)

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Delete App Data: Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes all local SQLite databases and saved images instantly.
  • Delete Cloud Backups: You can delete your backup file anytime directly from the Settings screen inside Split Trip or via your Google Drive Account settings.
  • Account Deletion: Since we do not maintain central remote database servers storing your trip details, deleting local data wipes your information permanently.

07 Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Children's Privacy: Split Trip is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Changes: We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this webpage with a revised "Effective Date".
